How we'll work together

Therapy that meets you where you actually are

You don't need to know the "right" technique before you walk in. I draw from a few well-researched approaches and shape sessions around what's actually happening in your life — here's what that can look like in plain terms.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

When your thoughts feel like they're running the show, CBT helps you notice the patterns, question them, and build steadier ways of responding — so anxious spirals stop feeling automatic.

Mindfulness-Based Approaches

For when your mind is everywhere but the present. We build small, realistic mindfulness habits that lower reactivity and help you feel more in your body, not just stuck in your head.

Person-Centered Therapy

Sometimes you don't need advice — you need to be heard without judgement. This approach puts your pace and your language first, and trusts you to lead the process.

Solution-Focused Brief Therapy

When you need traction, not just talk. We focus on what's already working for you and map out concrete, realistic next steps — especially useful for time-bound or workplace stress.

Workplace stress & burnout · Student and academic pressure · The specific anxieties of being early-career and Gen Z — career pressure, comparison, and digital burnout.

A little about my background

Training grounded in real people, not just theory

I hold a Master's in Counselling Psychology from Loyola College, Chennai. My training years were spent across clinical, school, and foundation settings — sitting with real people navigating real pressure, which shaped how I practise far more than any textbook did.

Alongside coursework, I completed over 30 hours of pro-bono client work during my practicum, and researched how purpose and social connection shape wellbeing at work — a thread that still informs how I think about stress, burnout, and identity today.

Outside client work, I've spent my student years building peer-support systems — from founding an alumni mentorship network to leading student wellbeing initiatives at college. I care about the same thing in both spaces: making support easier to reach.
